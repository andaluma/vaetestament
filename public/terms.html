2. Our role - what we do and do not do

We are not a law firm. UAE Property Wills provides guidance, preparation, translation and coordination for registering a will with the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) Wills Service for real estate in the United Arab Emirates (UAE). We do not provide legal advice and do not act as lawyers.

What we do:

  • Translate your situation and wishes into concrete instructions for the DIFC portal
  • Guide you step by step through the registration process
  • Inform you about what the DIFC portal requires and how to avoid common pitfalls
  • For Tier 2 (Guided Registration): review the portal entries together in a live video session of approximately 40 minutes
  • For Tier 3 (Full Service): connect you with a qualified DIFC lawyer who provides the legal expertise, and coordinate your process

What we do not do:

  • Provide legal advice about your specific situation
  • Draft or sign your will on your behalf
  • Make decisions about beneficiaries, executors or distribution for you
  • Guarantee the outcome of proceedings at DIFC, Dubai Land Department or other authorities

For substantive legal advice, we refer you to a qualified DIFC lawyer. For Tier 3, we arrange that introduction for you.
