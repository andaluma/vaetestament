## Two types of DIFC will

The DIFC offers two types of will: the Property Will and the Full Will. The difference is not legal validity, as both are validly registered with the DIFC. The difference is what they cover.

A **Property Will** covers only real estate held in your personal name. This includes apartments, villas and other property in all seven Emirates. For property in Dubai, the DIFC court's decision can be enforced. For property in Abu Dhabi, an application at the Abu Dhabi court follows after the DIFC procedure before the property can be transferred. For many foreign owners, property is the only thing they own in the UAE.

A **Full Will** covers everything a Property Will covers, plus other assets and provisions: bank accounts, investments, vehicles, guardianship of minor children, and property held through a company structure.

## When is a Property Will enough?

A Property Will is the right choice if all of the following apply:

- Your property is in your personal name (not through a company, holding or free zone company)
- You own at most 5 properties in the UAE
- You only want to include property, no other assets
- You do not need to arrange guardianship for minor children in the will

If all of these apply, a Property Will is almost always enough. You pay lower DIFC registration fees and you do not need a lawyer.

## When do you need a Full Will?

A Full Will is needed in the following situations.

### Property held through a company

If your property is registered to a company, holding, free zone company or other legal entity, it falls outside a Property Will. Your heirs then inherit the shares or interest in the entity, not the property itself. That requires a Full Will with legal guidance.

### Assets other than property

Do you also have bank accounts, investments or vehicles in the UAE? A Property Will covers real estate only. Other assets need a Full Will.

### Guardianship of minor children

Do you want to set out who becomes guardian of your minor children if both parents pass away? A guardianship provision can only be included in a Full Will.

### More than 5 properties

With more than 5 properties, the distribution becomes complex enough to justify legal guidance. The risk of mistakes grows with the number of properties and beneficiaries.

## What does the difference cost?

The cost of a DIFC will has two parts: the guidance fee and the DIFC registration fees.

**Property Will (Guided Registration, Tier 2):**
- Guidance fee: EUR 797, including a guided session
- DIFC registration fees: AED 7,500 (single will) or AED 10,000 (mirror wills for spouses)

**Full Will (Fully Managed, Tier 3):**
- Guidance fee: EUR 1,499
- DIFC registration fees: AED 10,000 (single) or AED 15,000 (mirror)
- DIFC lawyer's fee: typically AED 5,000 to 10,000

The difference in total cost is significant. Do not pay for a Full Will if a Property Will covers your situation.
