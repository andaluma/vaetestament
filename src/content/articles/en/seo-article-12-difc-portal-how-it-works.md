## The portal exists, and that is good news

The DIFC Wills Service Centre has an online portal where you can register your will. You do not need to travel to Dubai, and you do not need a lawyer for a Property Will. You can handle everything digitally.

That is the good news. The less good news: the portal was not designed with the average foreign property owner in mind.

## What you will run into

### Legal choices without context

The portal asks you to make decisions that assume legal knowledge. Who becomes executor? What if a beneficiary dies before you? The portal offers little explanation for these questions.

### Documents in a specific format

You upload documents in a specific format. Passports must show the photo page, in colour. Title deeds must be current. Wrong files mean delays.

### No phone support

The DIFC offers support by email. There is no phone number to call if you get stuck, and response times vary.

## The steps in the portal

Registration through the portal follows these steps:

**1. Create an account**

You register on the DIFC Wills Service Centre portal with your email address, receive a confirmation and log in.

**2. Choose the type of will**

You choose between a Property Will and a Full Will, and between a single will or mirror wills (for spouses).

**3. Enter personal details**

Your full name (as in your passport), date of birth, nationality, address and contact details. For mirror wills, also those of your spouse.

**4. Add your property**

For each property: the address, the plot number and the emirate. You upload the title deed.

**5. Name beneficiaries and an executor**

You name the beneficiaries per property and set the distribution. You also appoint an executor who carries out the will after your death.

**6. Upload documents**

Passport (colour copy of the photo page), title deeds, and the passports of your witnesses. Files must meet the specifications.

**7. Review, book and pay**

You check all details, confirm everything is correct, book the signing appointment and pay the DIFC registration fees.

**8. Appointment and registration**

During the online appointment you confirm the will. It is then officially registered with the DIFC.

## What to prepare for the appointment

- **Witnesses:** you need two witnesses aged 18 or over, with a valid passport. They may not be a beneficiary, or the partner of a beneficiary. They join by video, from anywhere.
- **Two devices:** you need two devices, one of them with a touchscreen for the digital signature.
- **Duration:** the appointment takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

## Where it often goes wrong

The most common problems we see:

- **Wrong file formats** when uploading documents
- **Name spelling** that does not match the passport
- **Unclear choices about executor and beneficiaries**, so the form gets stuck
- **Plot numbers** that do not match the property papers
- **The wrong type of will** (a Property Will where a Full Will was needed)

These mistakes cost time. At best you resubmit; at worst the application is rejected and you start again.
