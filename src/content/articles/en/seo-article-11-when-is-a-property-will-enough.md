## Most owners only need a Property Will

Of all the property owners we speak to, most fit a Property Will. The reason is simple: they own one or two apartments in their personal name, without company structures or other UAE assets.

Yet many owners end up with a Full Will, often because they did not know the distinction exists, or because nobody pointed them to the simpler option.

## The checklist: does a Property Will fit you?

Answer the following four questions. If the answer to all four is "yes", a Property Will is almost certainly enough.

**1. Is your property in your personal name?**

Check the title deed. If your name is on it as owner, the answer is yes. If the property is registered to a company, holding or free zone company, the answer is no.

**2. Do you own at most 5 properties in the UAE?**

Count all apartments, villas and other properties. Up to and including 5 properties fit a Property Will. With more than 5, the distribution is complex enough to justify legal guidance.

**3. Do you only want to include property?**

A Property Will covers real estate only. If you also want to include bank accounts, investments, vehicles or other UAE assets, you need a Full Will.

**4. Do you not need guardianship provisions?**

If you have minor children and want to set out who becomes their guardian, that is only possible in a Full Will. A Property Will contains no guardianship provisions.

## Common misconceptions

### "I have two apartments, so I need a Full Will"

Not true. A Property Will covers up to 5 properties. Two apartments in your personal name is the most common situation and fits a Property Will.

### "I bought through an agent, so the property is not in my name"

In the vast majority of cases the property is registered in your personal name after purchase, even if you bought through an agent. Check the title deed.

### "I want my partner as beneficiary, isn't that complex?"

No. The most common distribution (everything to the partner, with the children as substitute beneficiaries) is standard and fits a Property Will.

## Examples that fit a Property Will

- **Married couple, 1 apartment in Dubai Marina:** both spouses register mirror wills. Everything to the partner, children as substitute beneficiaries.
- **Married couple, 2 apartments in Dubai and Abu Dhabi:** the same set-up, with two properties divided.
- **Single owner, 1 villa in Abu Dhabi:** a single will with a sibling as beneficiary.
