## The question everyone asks

"Do I need a lawyer for my DIFC will?" The answer depends on your situation, and it is "no" more often than you might think.

## When you do not need a lawyer

A **DIFC Property Will** does not require a lawyer. The DIFC explicitly allows you to register a Property Will yourself through the online portal. You choose your beneficiaries, your executor and how your property is divided, and the portal takes you through the process.

This applies if:

- Your property is in your personal name
- You include at most 5 properties
- You include only property (no bank accounts, vehicles or other assets)
- You do not need guardianship provisions

In that situation, guidance is enough. A guidance service such as UAE Property Wills takes you through the portal, checks your documents and makes sure everything is entered correctly. We do not give legal advice and do not draft wills, and you do not need that here.

## When you do need a lawyer

A DIFC-registered lawyer is needed for a **Full Will**. That is the case if:

- Your property is held through a company, holding or free zone company
- You want to include other UAE assets besides property
- You want to arrange guardianship of minor children
- You own more than 5 properties
- Your distribution is legally complex (several generations, conditional provisions)

In these situations the lawyer drafts the will, reviews the ownership structure and makes sure the legal provisions are worded correctly. We coordinate the process and refer you to DIFC-registered lawyers in our network.

## The difference in cost

The difference is not only legal but also financial.

**Without a lawyer (Property Will, Guided Registration):**
- Guidance fee: EUR 797
- DIFC registration fees: AED 7,500 (single will) or AED 10,000 (mirror wills)
- No lawyer's fees

**With a lawyer (Full Will, Fully Managed):**
- Guidance fee: EUR 1,499
- DIFC registration fees: AED 10,000 (single) or AED 15,000 (mirror)
- DIFC lawyer's fee: typically AED 5,000 to 10,000

The total difference can reach EUR 5,000 or more. Do not pay for legal expertise you do not need.
