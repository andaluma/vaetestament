The problem

The UAE applies Sharia inheritance law - even for you.

You are European. You bought an apartment in Dubai or Abu Dhabi as an investment, perhaps through a project you found from home. You thought carefully about the returns - but probably not about what happens to that apartment when you pass away.

The UAE applies Sharia law by default to all property on its territory at the time of death. That means your wishes are not legally binding. Your partner, children, or whoever you consider your heir - they are legally last in line if nothing is formalised.

A DIFC Will changes that. It is the only way for non-Muslims to maintain legal control over who inherits your UAE property.

12,5%

What your partner inherits without a will.

Say you own a €400,000 apartment in Dubai. You die unexpectedly. Without a DIFC Will, your partner receives €50,000. The remaining €350,000 is distributed among family members under Sharia law - possibly people you would never have chosen as heirs.

The solution

What is a DIFC Will, and why do you need one?

Het Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) heeft een aparte juridische structuur die niet-moslims toestaat een testament te registreren dat gebaseerd is op westerse erfrechtnormen. Dit testament is geldig voor al uw vastgoed én andere bezittingen in de UAE.

De registratie verloopt via een officieel online portaal. U hoeft niet fysiek in Dubai aanwezig te zijn.

  • Your partner inherits 100% if that is your wish - not 12.5%
  • You decide who the guardians are for minor children
  • Geldig voor vastgoed in alle Emirates
  • Amendable during your lifetime
  • Registration fully possible remotely
